# LocalCanon contributor instructions

- Read `docs/PROJECT_BRIEF.md` before changing product scope.
- Read `docs/SHARED_DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md` before interface or design changes. Let content determine structure; avoid decorative one-sided accent borders.
- Preserve the existing `docs/references/bandung-moodboard.png` as reference material.
- Keep region-specific facts separate from country-wide or neighbouring-region facts.
- Do not invent local artists, songs, listings, events or community contributions.
- Record sources for factual entries; mark editorial proposals as research until validated.
- Keep traditional music visible alongside contemporary artists, with the traditional spotlight first.
- Do not embed unlicensed audio, photography or artwork. Track creator, source and permission separately.
- Keep commercial listings and sponsorship distinct from editorial content.
- Run `npm run check` and `npm test` after functional changes. Check narrow-screen usability when changing the interface.
- Public GitHub/Pages publication was authorised by Richard on 1 October 2026. Preserve prototype limitations and image attribution in future releases.
